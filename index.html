Privacy Policy — UPSC Prelims PYQ Practice

Last updated: September 7, 2026

UPSC Prelims PYQ Practice is a browser extension that replaces the browser's New Tab page with a UPSC CSE Prelims previous-year-question practice interface.

Information stored

The extension stores practice-related information locally on the user's device, including question attempts, correct/incorrect results, bookmarks, settings, and basic practice statistics. This information is used only to provide the extension's study and progress features.

Information we do not collect

The current version does not request access to browsing history, website content, cookies, contacts, files, passwords, or personal communications. It does not require an account or email address.

Sharing

The current version does not transmit practice history or other extension-stored data to the developer or to third parties.

Permissions

The extension uses the browser's storage permission to save settings and practice progress, and the standard New Tab override to show the practice screen when a new tab is opened.

Data control

Users can reset stored progress from the extension's Settings panel. Removing the extension also removes its stored local extension data according to the browser's extension-data behavior.

Future changes

If a future version introduces accounts, analytics, cloud sync, advertising, payments, or other data collection/transmission, this policy will be updated before those practices are introduced and the store disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the support contact provided in the extension's store listing.
